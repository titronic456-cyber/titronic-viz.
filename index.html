Titronic Visualization

Turn your files into charts

Titronic Visualization turns data and documents into charts you can download. Your files stay on your device and are never uploaded to a server.

  1. Upload a CSV, Excel, JSON, PDF, Word or text file.
  2. Choose a chart type, the X axis and the values to plot.
  3. Download the chart as an image.

Spreadsheets and JSON plot your columns. PDF, Word and text files plot their most-used words. Group combines rows with the same label by sum, average or count.
